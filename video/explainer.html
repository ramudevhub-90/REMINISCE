How Reminisce works

A two-and-a-half minute walkthrough · Qloo Agentic Hackathon

A companion for dementia carers

Reminisce

Small, familiar joys, chosen for them.

An agent that uses Qloo’s taste graph to rebuild the music, films and food of someone’s youth, and gets better every session.

The problem

55M+

people live with dementia worldwide. Many can no longer say what they like, but familiar songs and tastes from their youth can still reach them.

“What do I even talk about with Dad today? I only know he loved Kishore Kumar.”

Step 4 · A ready-to-use session kit

Everything for today’s visit, in half a minute.

  • Music with photos and play links
  • Film clips from the years they grew up in
  • Something to cook together, and where it’s served
  • Conversation starters to open memories gently

Step 5 · Share it, one card at a time

The carer only notes what sparked.

Big photos and one simple prompt per card. A tap records the reaction: Lit up, A little or Not today, plus an optional note.

Step 6 · The weekly loop

Each session teaches the next one.

What lit them up returns as a favourite and goes back to Qloo as a new signal. What didn’t land is set aside. The kit gets more personal every week.

How it’s built

Web appAny browser: phone, tablet or laptop
AppSyncGuest sign-in, no account needed
SQS queueAgent jobs run in the background
Agent (Lambda)Plans the Qloo calls and builds the kit
Qloo Taste AIVia the qloo harness, cached in DynamoDB

Progress streams back to the browser step by step

Serverless on AWS. The Qloo key stays on the server; the person’s name is never sent to Qloo.

Why it needs Qloo, and what’s next

Across domains

A singer leads to films and food. Only a taste graph connects them.

Learns without words

Reactions become signals for someone who can no longer say what they like.

Honest evidence

Every pick shows its Qloo affinity, framed as a group pattern, never a claim about the person.

Also inside: group sessions for care homes, a timeline of the years they grew up in, and a taste portrait. Next: a voice debrief and a profile the whole family can share.
